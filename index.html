How it works
Designed to catch stocks before they enter the Pre-Flight Breakout (PFB) phase. The screener compares a rolling 7-day evaluation window against the previous 7 days. It strictly isolates assets where average trading volume and price surge past configured multipliers, combined with a continuous 3-day rising price action.
