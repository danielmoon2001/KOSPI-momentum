Technical indicators & filter rules

Prior closes define the channel. A close outside it marks a breakout.

Long: a rise of x% from the reference low. Short: a fall of x% from the reference high. Running extrema reset at each position change; rolling extrema use the previous d closes. Smaller moves are ignored. A y% reversal can exit to neutral (y < x), with re-entry evaluated on a later observation. Minimum hold suppresses signals until h observations after entry; “Exit after h” instead closes at that point. Long/short refer to the foreign currency against KRW.

Momentum.

Korea & US Equity breakout screeners.
Designed by Moon Quixote.

How it works
The screen identifies stocks with three consecutively rising trading closes, evaluating average volume and the latest close against the preceding five-calendar-day base. Both volume and price thresholds must be exceeded.

Matches are then tracked independently of screening criteria while closing prices hold or rise. Tracking ends at the first lower close, with the full price history retained on view through that market session.
